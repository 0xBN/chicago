How Google Maps works

“Directions” opens Google Maps with the venue as the destination and your current location as the starting point (if location access is allowed). Scheduled Saturday links default to walking; Sunday schedule links default to driving. Nearby recommendations use walking. You can switch modes in Maps.

Times are targets, not reservations. Queues, closures and final admissions may change. Approximate distances use stored coordinates (straight line, not street routes). Location is processed on your device and is not saved by this page. Parking, food, and optional purchases may cost money; site entry is free under Open House participation.
